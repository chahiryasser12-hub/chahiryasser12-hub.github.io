What this calculator does

This calculator projects how a starting balance and regular contributions may grow when returns are compounded.

Formula used

Future value of a lump sum: FV = P(1 + r/n)^(nt). Regular contributions are added using the selected contribution timing.

Worked example

$10,000 growing at 5% compounded monthly for 10 years becomes about $16,470 before tax and fees, without extra deposits.

How to interpret the result

The projection separates money contributed from estimated growth, helping show how rate and time affect the result.

Important limitations

The rate is assumed constant and is not guaranteed. Inflation, taxes, investment fees, market losses and contribution timing can materially change actual results. Currency is only a display unit.

Frequently asked questions

How often is interest compounded?

+
The selected frequency determines how often the annual rate is applied during the year.

Are monthly contributions included?

+
Yes, when entered. Contribution timing can slightly change the final value.

Does this include tax, fees or inflation?

+
No. Those factors can materially reduce real-world purchasing power and returns.

Is the projected return guaranteed?

+
No. It is a mathematical scenario, not an investment forecast.
