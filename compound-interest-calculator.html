What is compound interest?

Compound interest is interest earning interest. Your returns start earning their own returns, so the balance snowballs — slowly at first, then dramatically. This calculator compounds monthly and lets you add a regular monthly contribution to model steady investing.

Why starting early wins

Time matters more than the amount. Starting five years earlier can leave you with substantially more. Once you know your target, our savings goal calculator works out how much to set aside each month to reach it.

Three things that boost growth

  • Time — every extra year matters more.
  • Rate of return — even 1% higher compounds hugely.
  • Consistency — regular contributions add fuel.
