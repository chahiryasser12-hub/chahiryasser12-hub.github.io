How Much Should You Save Each Month?

📅 July 2026⏱ 6 min readBy Fynzo

Saving feels vague until you attach a number to it. Here's how to find yours.

Start with a goal and a deadline

A goal without a date is a wish. Decide the amount and when you need it, then work backwards. The savings goal calculator tells you the exact monthly amount, including interest.

The 50/30/20 rule

A simple budget: 50% needs, 30% wants, 20% savings. On a $2,500 monthly take-home, that's $500 saved.
