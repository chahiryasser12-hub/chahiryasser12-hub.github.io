BMI vs Body Fat Percentage: What Each Estimate Means

August 1, 2026Reviewed by Yasser Chahir

BMI uses height and weight, while body-fat formulas attempt to estimate body composition from measurements. Both have limitations.

BMI

Fast screening ratio based only on height and weight.

Body-fat estimate

Uses measurements and an equation, but tape placement and formula error affect the result.

Best use

Use repeated measurements consistently for general trend awareness, not diagnosis.

Practical takeaway

Neither result diagnoses health. Children, pregnancy, medical conditions and athletic body composition require appropriate context and qualified guidance.
