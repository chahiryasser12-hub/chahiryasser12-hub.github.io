How to Calculate Your BMI (and What It Really Means)

📅 July 2026⏱ 6 min readBy Fynzo

BMI is the most common health number in the world — but most people don't know what it tells them.

The BMI formula

BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)²

Someone 175 cm and 72 kg has a BMI of 23.5, in the healthy range.

What the categories mean

BMICategory
Below 18.5Underweight
18.5–24.9Healthy
25–29.9Overweight
30+Obese

The BMI calculator also shows your healthy weight range.

Why BMI isn't the whole story

BMI doesn't tell muscle from fat. A muscular athlete can read 'overweight' while lean. Pair it with our body fat calculator.
