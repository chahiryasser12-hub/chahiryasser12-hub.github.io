# Static numerical search examples added

Search engines cannot rely on every visitor-generated JavaScript result as a separate indexable answer. Therefore, static HTML examples were added to the five priority calculator pages.

- Mortgage: four examples covering $300,000, 20% down, 15 vs 30 years, and 5.5% vs 6.5%.
- Loan: three examples covering $20,000 for 60 months, $10,000 for 36 months, and 36 vs 60 months.
- BMI: three metric examples, including 70 kg and 175 cm.
- Calories: two adult Mifflin-St Jeor examples plus an interpretation warning.
- Compound interest: three examples with starting balances, monthly contributions, rates and terms.

All examples state the assumptions and avoid creating thin number-swapped URLs.
