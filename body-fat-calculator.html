What this calculator does

This calculator estimates body-fat percentage from body measurements using a circumference-based equation.

Formula used

The U.S. Navy-style method uses logarithmic relationships between height and selected circumferences; the exact equation differs by sex.

Worked example

For an illustrative set of measurements, the calculator may estimate 20%; this means roughly 20% of total body mass is estimated as fat, not that exactly 20% has been measured.

How to interpret the result

Use repeated measurements under similar conditions to observe a trend rather than relying on one reading.

Important limitations

Tape placement and measurement error can materially affect the result. Circumference equations are estimates and are not equivalent to DEXA or clinical assessment.

Source and review information

Method reference: CDC: limits of indirect body-composition screening. Fynzo explains and implements the calculation independently; the linked organization does not endorse this website.

Where should I measure?

+
