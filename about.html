About Fynzo

Clear tools, transparent methods.

Who maintains Fynzo, how its calculators work, and what their results mean.

Fynzo is an independent online project created and maintained by Yasser Chahir in Morocco.

Why I created Fynzo

I created Fynzo to make everyday financial, health and general calculations easier to understand. Many online calculators provide a number without clearly explaining how it was calculated or what the result means.

Fynzo aims to offer simple, fast and transparent tools that anyone can use without creating an account.

How the calculators are reviewed

Each calculator is tested with sample values and checked against its documented formula. When a tool is updated, its inputs, calculation logic and displayed results are reviewed for consistency.

Reasonable care is taken to keep the tools useful and accurate, but errors can still occur. If you notice an incorrect result or an unclear explanation, please contact us so it can be reviewed.

Formulas and sources

Fynzo uses commonly accepted mathematical formulas and publicly available reference information. Where applicable, each calculator page explains the formula, assumptions and units used.

Health calculators are general screening and educational tools. Financial calculators provide estimates based on the values entered by the user. They cannot account for every personal, medical, legal, tax or financial circumstance.

Educational purpose and limitations

All calculators, articles and results on Fynzo are provided for general educational and informational purposes only.

Fynzo does not provide medical, financial, tax or legal advice. A result should not be treated as a diagnosis, a professional recommendation, a loan offer or a guarantee of future outcomes. For important decisions, verify the information and consult a qualified professional.

Privacy

Most calculations are performed directly in the user's browser, and registration is not required to use the calculators. For more information, read our Privacy Policy.
