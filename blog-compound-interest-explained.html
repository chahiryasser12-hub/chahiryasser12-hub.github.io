Compound Interest Explained (With Real Examples)

📅 July 2026⏱ 6 min readBy Fynzo

Compound interest is the closest thing to magic in personal finance. Here's how it really works.

What is compound interest?

Compound interest is interest earning interest. Your returns start earning their own returns, so the balance snowballs — slowly at first, then dramatically.

A real example

Invest $5,000 plus $200/month at 8%:

AfterYou put inIt's worth
10 years$29,000~$44,000
20 years$53,000~$142,000
30 years$77,000~$343,000

Try your own numbers in the compound interest calculator.

Why starting early wins

Someone investing $200/month from age 25 often ends up with more than someone investing $400/month from 40. Time does the heavy lifting.

💡 The best time to start was years ago. The second best is today.

Three boosters

  • Time — every extra year matters more.
  • Rate — even 1% higher compounds hugely.
  • Consistency — regular contributions add fuel.
