How the temperature converter works

The calculator converts your value into Celsius first, then into your target scale using the standard formulas. 0C = 32F = 273.15 K (freezing), 100C = 212F (boiling), and 37C is about 98.6F (body temperature). For distances and sizes, our length converter handles metric and imperial units.

When you'll need it

Cooking with a foreign recipe, checking the weather abroad, or reading a science problem all mix temperature scales. This tool bridges them, and it works offline once installed as an app.
