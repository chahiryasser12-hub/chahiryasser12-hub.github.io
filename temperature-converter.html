Temperature Converter

Use this free temperature converter for a clear, instant answer. Enter your numbers and the result updates immediately — no sign-up, everything private in your browser.

What this calculator does

This tool converts between Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin using standard linear relationships.

Formula used

°F = °C × 9/5 + 32; °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9; K = °C + 273.15.

Worked example

20°C equals 68°F and 293.15 K.

How to interpret the result

Converted values represent the same temperature in different scales.

Important limitations

Displayed results may be rounded. Kelvin cannot be below absolute zero, 0 K.
