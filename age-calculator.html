Age Calculator

Use this free age calculator for a clear, instant answer. Enter your numbers and the result updates immediately — no sign-up, everything private in your browser.

What this calculator does

This tool calculates elapsed calendar time between a birth date and a selected date.

Formula used

Age is found by subtracting calendar dates and adjusting years, months and days when the later month or day has not yet been reached.

Worked example

Someone born on July 15, 2000 is 26 years and 16 days old on July 31, 2026.

How to interpret the result

The result expresses completed calendar years plus the remaining months and days.

Important limitations

Calendar month lengths and leap years affect the day count. Legal age may depend on local rules and the time zone used.
