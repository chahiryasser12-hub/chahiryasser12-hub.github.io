Quick answer

Tip calculator gives an instant estimate from the values entered above. Use the result as a starting point and review the assumptions and limits below.

How to use the calculator

Enter values in one currency and period, then change one assumption at a time.

Assumptions

The estimate uses the entered rates and excludes unspecified fees, taxes, timing rules or market changes.

Common mistakes

  • Mixing monthly and annual values
  • Ignoring fees or taxes
  • Comparing scenarios with different bases
  • Rounding too early

What this calculator does

This calculator adds a chosen tip percentage to a bill and can divide the total among people.

Formula used

Tip = bill × tip rate ÷ 100. Total = bill + tip. Per person = total ÷ number of people.

Worked example

A $100 bill with a 15% tip totals $115; split among five people, that is $23 each.

How to interpret the result

The result separates the tip, total and equal share per person.

Important limitations

Taxes, service charges, unequal splitting and local tipping customs are not automatically included. Currency is a display unit.
