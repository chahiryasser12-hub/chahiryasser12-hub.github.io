Master Percentages: Discounts, Tips & Tax Made Easy

📅 July 2026⏱ 6 min readBy Fynzo

Percentages run daily life — sales, tips, tax, interest. Here's how to handle them in your head.

The core idea

A percentage is a fraction of 100. To find X% of a number, multiply by X and divide by 100. The percentage calculator does it instantly.

Quick mental tricks

  • 10% — move the decimal one place left.
  • 5% — half of 10%.
  • 20% — double 10%.
  • 15% tip — 10% + half of that.

Shopping math

A '25% off' sticker and tax at checkout change the real price. Use the discount calculator for the sale price and the sales tax calculator for the total. Splitting a meal? The tip calculator handles it.

💡 Two 20% discounts aren't 40% off — apply them one after another (36% off).
