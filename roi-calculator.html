How ROI is calculated

ROI = (final value minus amount invested) divided by amount invested, times 100. A positive number is a gain; negative is a loss. Because it is a percentage, ROI lets you compare very different investments on equal footing.

What ROI does not show

Plain ROI ignores time - a 20% return in one year beats 20% over ten years. For long horizons, our compound interest calculator models growth year by year.
