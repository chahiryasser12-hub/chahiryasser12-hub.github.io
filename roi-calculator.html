How ROI is calculated

ROI = (final value − amount invested) ÷ amount invested × 100. A positive number is a gain; a negative one is a loss. Because it is a percentage, ROI lets you compare very different investments on equal footing.

What ROI does not show

Plain ROI ignores time — a 20% return in one year beats 20% over ten years. For long horizons, our compound interest calculator models growth year by year.

When to use it

  • Comparing two investment options quickly.
  • Checking whether a purchase paid off.
  • Evaluating business or marketing spend.
