What this calculator does

ROI compares the gain or loss from an investment with its original cost.

Formula used

ROI = (final value − initial cost) ÷ initial cost × 100.

Worked example

An investment of $1,000 that becomes $1,200 has a gain of $200 and an ROI of 20%.

How to interpret the result

A positive ROI indicates a gain and a negative ROI indicates a loss. ROI alone does not show how long the investment was held.

Important limitations

Taxes, fees, cash flows, inflation, risk and holding period are excluded unless you include them in the values. ROI is not a forecast or investment recommendation.

Frequently asked questions

What does a positive ROI mean?

+
The final value is higher than the original amount before considering omitted costs.

Does ROI account for time?

+
No. The same ROI over one year and ten years represents very different performance.

Are fees and taxes included?

+
Only if you include them in the amounts entered.
