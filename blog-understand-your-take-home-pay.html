Gross vs Net: Understand Your Take-Home Pay

📅 July 2026⏱ 6 min readBy Fynzo

Your salary and your bank deposit are two different numbers. Here's why — and how to plan around net.

Gross vs net

Gross is what you earn before tax. Net — your take-home — is what actually lands in your account. For budgeting, net is the only number that matters.

What is an effective tax rate?

It's your total tax ÷ total income — usually lower than your top bracket. Find yours on a payslip, then enter it in the take-home pay calculator.

Budget from net

  • Use monthly net as the top of your budget.
  • Keep housing under about a third of it.
  • Save a slice of every paycheck automatically.
