# SEO monitoring and monthly growth plan

## Weekly, 30 minutes
- Search Console: clicks, impressions, CTR, position, queries and pages.
- Page indexing: newly indexed, crawled not indexed and excluded URLs.
- GA4: organic sessions, engagement, calculator_result and article_to_calculator.
- GitHub Actions: confirm publishing and quality checks passed.

## Monthly review
1. Export Search Console page and query data.
2. Improve pages ranking between positions 8 and 30.
3. Rewrite titles only where impressions are meaningful and CTR is weak.
4. Add examples or FAQs based on real queries.
5. Check Core Web Vitals and mobile usability.
6. Review backlinks, referral traffic and social posts.
7. Publish the next four high-quality articles.

## Initial targets
- At least 80% of important pages indexed.
- Four reviewed articles per month.
- Five relevant referring domains during the first 90 days.
- Growing non-brand impressions.
- Organic CTR generally between 2% and 6%, interpreted by query and position.
- One documented technical and content review each month.
