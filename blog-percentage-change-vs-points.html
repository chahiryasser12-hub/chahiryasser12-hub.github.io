Percentage Change vs Percentage Points

August 1, 2026Reviewed by Yasser Chahir

Percentage change measures relative movement, while percentage points measure the arithmetic difference between two percentage values.

Percentage points

A rise from 20% to 25% is 5 percentage points.

Percentage change

The same rise is a 25% relative increase because 5 divided by 20 equals 25%.

Use the right label

Reporting a percentage increase as percentage points can materially change the meaning.

Practical takeaway

Always identify the original value and whether the question asks for a relative change or a direct difference.
