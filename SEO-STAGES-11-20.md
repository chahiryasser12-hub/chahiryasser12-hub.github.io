# Fynzo SEO stages 11 through 20

## 11. Topic Hubs and Pillar Pages
Five hub pages are maintained and refreshed after article publication. CollectionPage and ItemList data are added to the hubs.

## 12. Blog SEO Optimization
Articles retain unique metadata, Article schema, breadcrumbs, source sections, reviewer information, related links and optimized social previews.

## 13. Automated Article Publishing
The single production flow is `scheduled_posts.json` → `weekly_publisher.py` → hubs → SEO normalization → full audit → commit.

## 14. Canonical URL Management
Every indexable page has one self-referencing HTTPS canonical. Open Graph URL matches the canonical.

## 15. Open Graph and Social Media Previews
All pages have complete Open Graph and Twitter Card metadata with 1200 x 630 preview dimensions and image alt text.

## 16. Image SEO Optimization
Images receive alt attributes, dimensions when detectable, async decoding and appropriate eager or lazy loading. Twenty-three optimized 1200 x 630 social preview PNG files are included.

## 17. Google Analytics 4 Event Tracking
Privacy-safe events include calculator_result, copy_result, copy_share_link, download_result, print_pdf, related_tool_click, article_to_calculator, contact_submit and helpful_vote. Calculator inputs and results are never sent.

## 18. Google Search Console Setup
The site is technically ready. `SEARCH-CONSOLE-SETUP.md` explains owner-only verification, sitemap and monitoring steps.

## 19. Accessibility and Semantic HTML
Skip links, main targets, navigation labels, live result regions, form labels, keyboard FAQ controls, image alt text and reduced-motion support are included.

## 20. Mobile SEO and Core Web Vitals
Responsive image rules, 16px mobile form controls, horizontal table overflow, 44px action targets, native fonts, async images, optimized social assets and mobile layout safeguards are included.
