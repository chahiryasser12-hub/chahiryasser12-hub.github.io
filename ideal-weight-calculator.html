How it is calculated

It uses the Devine formula to estimate an ideal weight from height and sex, and shows the healthy range for a BMI of 18.5-24.9. Together they give a sensible target window rather than one rigid number.

Use it as a guide

Ideal weight is a guide, not a rule - muscle, frame and age all matter. For a quick screen use our bmi calculator, and to plan intake our calorie calculator.
