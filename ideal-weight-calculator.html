Ideal Weight for Height Calculator

Find a healthy target weight for your height. This calculator uses the Devine formula and also shows your healthy BMI-based weight range, so you get a realistic window rather than one number.

How it is calculated

It uses the Devine formula to estimate an ideal body weight from your height and sex, and also shows the healthy weight range for a BMI of 18.5–24.9. Together they give a sensible target window.

Why a range beats one number

Ideal weight is a guide, not a rule — muscle, frame and age all matter. For a quick screen of where you are now, use our BMI calculator, and to plan intake, our calorie calculator.

When to seek advice

  • If your weight is well outside the healthy range.
  • Before an intense diet or training program.
