Net worth formula

Net worth equals total assets minus total liabilities.

Use consistent values

Use the same valuation date and reasonable current values for assets and outstanding balances for debts.

Important limitation

Some assets are difficult to value and taxes or selling costs can affect realizable value.

Frequently asked questions

What counts as an asset?

+
Cash, investments, property and other items with measurable value can be included.

What counts as a liability?

+
Mortgages, loans, credit cards and other outstanding debts.

Can net worth be negative?

+
Yes, when liabilities exceed assets.

How often should I calculate it?

+
Periodic comparison can be useful if values and categories are kept consistent.
