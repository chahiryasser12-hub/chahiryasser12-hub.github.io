Break-Even Calculator

Calculate contribution margin, break-even units, break-even revenue and profit at a selected sales volume.

How to read this result

Result breakdown

A visual summary that updates with your calculation.

Break-even formula

Break-even units equal fixed costs divided by selling price minus variable cost per unit.

Contribution margin

Each unit contributes selling price minus variable cost toward fixed costs and then profit.

Important limitation

Actual costs and prices can change. The model assumes constant unit economics within the selected range.

Frequently asked questions

What if variable cost is higher than price?

+
The item has no positive contribution margin, so the simplified model cannot reach break-even.

Why round units up?

+
A fraction of a unit usually cannot be sold, so break-even units are rounded upward.

Does this include tax?

+
No, unless tax is already included in the entered costs.

Can I estimate profit?

+
Yes, enter expected units sold.
