Length Converter

Use this free length converter for a clear, instant answer. Enter your numbers and the result updates immediately — no sign-up, everything private in your browser.

What this calculator does

This tool converts a length by multiplying it by the fixed factor between the selected units.

Formula used

Converted value = input value × source-unit factor ÷ target-unit factor.

Worked example

1 metre equals 100 centimetres, approximately 3.28084 feet or 39.3701 inches.

How to interpret the result

The physical length does not change; only the unit used to express it changes.

Important limitations

Results may be rounded. Use sufficient precision for engineering, scientific or legal work.
