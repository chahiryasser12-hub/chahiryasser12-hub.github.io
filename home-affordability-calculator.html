How the estimate works

The calculator applies the selected housing DTI limit, subtracts entered debts and housing costs, then converts the remaining payment budget into a loan principal.

Use a range

Try several DTI limits and cost assumptions. A maximum approval amount is not necessarily a comfortable budget.

Important limitation

Lenders can use different DTI rules, credit standards, reserves, rates and property costs.

Frequently asked questions

What is DTI?

+
Debt-to-income ratio compares recurring monthly debt obligations with gross monthly income.

Does this guarantee approval?

+
No. It is an educational estimate.

Why include tax and insurance?

+
Those costs reduce the payment budget available for principal and interest.

Should I borrow the maximum?

+
Affordability also depends on savings, repairs, lifestyle and income stability.
