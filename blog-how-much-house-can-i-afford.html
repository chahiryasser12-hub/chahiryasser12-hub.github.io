How much house can I afford? The honest math

📅 30 July 2026⏱ 8 min readBy Fynzo

Before you fall in love with a listing, run the numbers. The right home price isn’t the biggest loan a bank will approve — it’s the payment you can comfortably live with.

The quick answer: the 28/36 rule

  • 28% — housing cost under 28% of gross monthly income.
  • 36% — all debts combined under 36%.

💡 A home priced 3–4× your annual income is usually affordable.

Step 1: Down payment

  • 20% down avoids PMI.
  • 10% down works but expect PMI.
  • 3–5% down possible but higher monthly cost.

Step 2: PITI

PartWhat it is
PrincipalThe loan you repay
InterestCost of borrowing

InsuranceHome insurance (+PMI if under 20%)

Step 3: Hidden costs

  • Closing costs (2–5%)
  • Maintenance (~1%/yr)
  • Moving & furnishing
  • Emergency fund

Step 4: Watch total interest

On a 30-year loan, interest can exceed half the price again. A 15-year term saves a fortune.
