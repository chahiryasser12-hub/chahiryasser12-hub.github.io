What this calculator does

This calculator estimates basal metabolic rate and applies an activity factor to approximate daily energy expenditure.

Formula used

Mifflin–St Jeor BMR: men = 10W + 6.25H − 5A + 5; women = 10W + 6.25H − 5A − 161, using kg, cm and years. Estimated maintenance calories = BMR × activity factor.

Worked example

A 30-year-old man at 80 kg and 180 cm has an estimated BMR of 1,780 kcal/day before activity is applied.

How to interpret the result

The result is a starting estimate for maintenance, not an exact daily requirement.

Important limitations

Metabolism, activity and health conditions vary. The estimate is not intended for children, pregnancy or breastfeeding and should not replace medical or dietetic advice.

Source and review information

Method reference: NIH/NIDDK Body Weight Planner. Fynzo explains and implements the calculation independently; the linked organization does not endorse this website.

Frequently asked questions

What are maintenance calories?

+
They are the estimated calories needed to keep body weight stable at the selected activity level.

How should I choose activity level?

+
Choose the level that best represents a normal week, not your most active day.

Is this suitable during pregnancy or breastfeeding?

+
No. Those situations have specific needs that should be discussed with a qualified professional.

Why might my real needs differ?

+
Metabolism, movement, health, sleep and tracking accuracy vary between people.
