BMR and TDEE explained

BMR is what you burn at rest. Multiply by an activity factor for TDEE. Uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation.

Setting your goal

Eat ~500 below TDEE to lose ~1 lb/week, at TDEE to maintain, or 300–500 above to gain.

FAQ

Which formula does it use? +
The Mifflin-St Jeor equation.
Is a calorie deficit safe? +
Moderate deficits are generally fine; avoid extreme cuts.
