Maintenance calorie examples for adults

These examples use the Mifflin-St Jeor BMR equation and common activity multipliers. They are general starting estimates, not personal dietary prescriptions.

Daily movement, formula error, food tracking, sleep, health and changing routines can make real energy needs differ from the estimate.

Quick answer

This calculator gives an instant estimate from the values entered above. Use the result as a planning starting point, then review the assumptions and limitations.

How to use the calculator

Enter the requested values using consistent units. Change one input at a time when comparing scenarios, and verify every value before using the result.

Assumptions

The estimate uses the values currently entered and the formula described on this page. Any fees, taxes, timing rules or personal circumstances not requested are excluded.

Common mistakes

  • Mixing monthly and annual values
  • Using inconsistent measurement units
  • Changing several inputs at once when comparing scenarios
  • Treating an estimate as a quote, approval, diagnosis or guarantee

What this calculator does

This calculator estimates basal metabolic rate and applies an activity factor to approximate daily energy expenditure.

Formula used

Mifflin–St Jeor BMR: men = 10W + 6.25H − 5A + 5; women = 10W + 6.25H − 5A − 161, using kg, cm and years. Estimated maintenance calories = BMR × activity factor.

Worked example

A 30-year-old man at 80 kg and 180 cm has an estimated BMR of 1,780 kcal/day before activity is applied.

How to interpret the result

The result is a starting estimate for maintenance, not an exact daily requirement.

Important limitations

Metabolism, activity and health conditions vary. The estimate is not intended for children, pregnancy or breastfeeding and should not replace medical or dietetic advice.

Frequently asked questions

What are maintenance calories?

+
They are the estimated calories needed to keep body weight stable at the selected activity level.

How should I choose activity level?

+
Choose the level that best represents a normal week, not your most active day.

Is this suitable during pregnancy or breastfeeding?

+
No. Those situations have specific needs that should be discussed with a qualified professional.

Why might my real needs differ?

+
Metabolism, movement, health, sleep and tracking accuracy vary between people.
