Take-Home Pay Calculator (Net Salary After Tax)

Get a quick estimate of your take-home pay. Enter your gross annual income and your effective tax rate to see what actually lands in your pocket each year and month.

What this calculator does

This calculator converts gross income into an estimated net amount using the tax rate entered by the user.

Formula used

Estimated tax = gross income × effective tax rate. Estimated net income = gross income − estimated tax.

Worked example

With gross income of $50,000 and an effective rate of 20%, estimated tax is $10,000 and estimated net income is $40,000.

How to interpret the result

The result shows the effect of the selected effective rate, not a complete tax return.

Important limitations

It does not determine brackets, deductions, credits, social contributions, local taxes or filing status. Tax rules vary by country and year. Currency is a display unit and the result is not tax advice.
