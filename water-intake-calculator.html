How Much Water Should I Drink a Day? (Water Intake Calculator)

Find your daily hydration target in seconds. Enter your weight and how much you exercise to see how much water you should drink per day, shown in litres, cups and ounces.

What this calculator does

This calculator gives a general hydration estimate from body weight and activity inputs.

Formula used

The tool applies its displayed weight-based baseline and adds the selected activity adjustment.

Worked example

If the selected baseline is 35 mL/kg, a 70 kg adult starts at about 2.45 litres before activity adjustments.

How to interpret the result

The result is a planning estimate and includes no clinical assessment of fluid balance.

Important limitations

Needs vary with climate, diet, pregnancy, illness, medicines, kidney or heart conditions and exercise. Excess water can also be harmful. Follow professional advice when fluid intake is medically restricted.

Source and review information

Method reference: National Academies: Dietary Reference Intakes for Water. Fynzo explains and implements the calculation independently; the linked organization does not endorse this website.
