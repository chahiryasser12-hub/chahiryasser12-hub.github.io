# Social media traffic plan

## Content system
Turn each calculator or article into four pieces:
1. A worked numerical example.
2. A common mistake and correction.
3. A short comparison or checklist.
4. A direct invitation to test the calculator.

## Weekly rhythm
- Monday: educational example on LinkedIn or Facebook.
- Wednesday: Pinterest visual or short comparison.
- Friday: new guide announcement.
- Weekend: one useful community answer when a relevant question exists.

## Tracking
Use page-specific links and review GA4 source/medium, engaged sessions, article-to-calculator clicks and calculator-result events. Do not judge success from impressions alone.

## Rules
- Do not paste the same promotional message into multiple communities.
- Do not disclose calculator inputs or results through analytics.
- Use the included 1200 x 630 social images.
- Prioritize the page that directly answers the post topic.
