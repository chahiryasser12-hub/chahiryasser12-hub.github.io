7 Ways to Pay Off a Loan Faster (and Save on Interest)

📅 July 2026⏱ 6 min readBy Fynzo

A loan can feel like a weight you carry for years. A few smart moves can lighten it dramatically.

Why paying faster saves so much

Interest is charged on your remaining balance. Shrink it faster and you pay less overall.

7 proven ways

1. Pay a little extra monthly

Even $50 extra goes straight to principal.

2. Make one extra payment a year

Bi-weekly payments create 13 full payments a year.

3. Round up

Owe $427? Pay $450.

4. Use windfalls

Tax refunds and bonuses are perfect lump sums.

5. Refinance to a lower rate

More of each payment attacks principal.

6. Keep the term short

Longer terms lower payments but raise total interest.

7. Target the highest rate first

The avalanche method saves the most.
