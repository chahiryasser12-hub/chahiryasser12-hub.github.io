Projection method

The calculator compounds the current balance and assumes equal end-of-month contributions at a constant rate.

Use scenarios

Test conservative, moderate and optimistic return assumptions instead of relying on one forecast.

Important limitation

Returns are not guaranteed. Fees, taxes, inflation, withdrawals and contribution changes can materially change the outcome.
