What this calculator does

This tool estimates the regular payment and total interest for an amortizing loan with equal monthly payments.

Formula used

M = P × [r(1+r)^n] ÷ [(1+r)^n − 1]. The annual rate is divided by 12 to obtain r, and years are multiplied by 12 to obtain n.

Worked example

A $10,000 loan at 8% a year for 3 years produces a payment of about $313 per month, before fees.

How to interpret the result

The payment repays interest and principal over the selected term. Compare total interest as well as the monthly amount.

Important limitations

Origination fees, late fees, insurance, taxes, prepayment charges and changing rates are excluded. The entered rate is not necessarily APR. Currency is a display choice. The result is not a credit offer or approval.

Frequently asked questions

Is the entered rate annual or monthly?

+
Enter the annual nominal interest rate. The calculator converts it to a monthly rate.

Does the payment include lender fees?

+
No. Origination fees, insurance, late fees and other charges are excluded.

What is the difference between rate and APR?

+
APR may include certain fees, while the calculator uses the interest rate entered.

Can I use this as a lender quote?

+
No. It is an educational estimate, not approval or an offer of credit.
