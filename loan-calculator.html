How the loan calculator works

It uses the standard amortizing-loan formula: each month your payment covers interest on the remaining balance, and the rest pays down principal. The result is a fixed monthly payment and a clear view of total interest. It works for a personal loan, an auto loan or a student loan.

Why total interest matters

Two loans can share the same monthly payment but very different total costs. A longer term lowers the monthly figure but raises total interest. If you are borrowing for a home instead, our mortgage calculator applies the same math to house prices and down payments.

Ways to pay less interest

  • Choose the shortest term you can afford.
  • Make small extra payments toward principal.
  • Shop around for a lower rate.
