Quick answer

Fuel Cost calculator gives an instant estimate from the values entered above. Use the result as a starting point and review the assumptions and limits below.

How to use the calculator

Enter values in one currency and period, then change one assumption at a time.

Assumptions

The estimate uses the entered rates and excludes unspecified fees, taxes, timing rules or market changes.

Common mistakes

  • Mixing monthly and annual values
  • Ignoring fees or taxes
  • Comparing scenarios with different bases
  • Rounding too early

What this calculator does

This calculator estimates fuel required and trip cost from distance, vehicle efficiency and fuel price.

Formula used

Fuel used = distance ÷ efficiency; trip cost = fuel used × price per unit. Unit conversions are applied when selected.

Worked example

A 500 km trip at 10 km/L uses about 50 L. At $1.50/L, estimated fuel cost is $75.

How to interpret the result

The result is a fuel-only estimate for the entered efficiency.

Important limitations

Traffic, weather, route, idling, load, driving style, tolls, maintenance and price changes are excluded. Ensure distance, efficiency and price units match.
