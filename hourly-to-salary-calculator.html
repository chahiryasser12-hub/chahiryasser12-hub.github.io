Hourly to Salary Calculator

Turn an hourly wage into a yearly salary in one step. Enter your hourly rate and the hours you work each week to instantly see your weekly, monthly and annual income.

What this calculator does

This tool converts an hourly rate into weekly, monthly and annual gross pay.

Formula used

Annual gross pay = hourly rate × paid hours per week × paid weeks per year.

Worked example

At $20 per hour, 40 hours a week and 52 paid weeks, estimated annual gross pay is $41,600.

How to interpret the result

The result is gross pay before deductions. Changing unpaid weeks or weekly hours can significantly change annual income.

Important limitations

Overtime premiums, bonuses, unpaid leave, taxes, benefits and irregular schedules are excluded unless reflected in the inputs. Currency is a display unit.

Source and review information
