Hourly to Salary Calculator

Turn an hourly wage into a yearly salary in one step. Enter your hourly rate and the hours you work each week to instantly see your weekly, monthly and annual income.

How the conversion works

This calculator multiplies your hourly rate by the number of hours you work each week, then by 52 weeks to give your annual salary. It also breaks the figure down into weekly and monthly amounts, so you can see your income at whatever scale is most useful for budgeting or comparing job offers.

It is perfect for freelancers, part-time workers, and anyone weighing an hourly role against a salaried one. Change the rate or hours and every number updates instantly.

Gross pay, not take-home

The result shows your gross income — the amount before tax and deductions. To estimate what actually lands in your bank account, run the annual figure through the take-home pay calculator, which applies your effective tax rate to reveal your net income.

Comparing hourly and salaried jobs

When comparing an hourly job to a salaried one, look beyond the headline number. A salaried role may include benefits, paid leave and predictable income, while an hourly role can offer flexibility and overtime. Convert both to the same annual figure first, then weigh the extras. This calculator gives you that apples-to-apples starting point.

Tips for an accurate estimate

  • Use your typical weekly hours, not your best or worst week.
  • Add overtime hours separately if they are regular.
  • Remember unpaid weeks (holidays) can lower the real annual total.
  • Convert to net pay before making big financial decisions.

Frequently asked questions

Does it include overtime?

+
No — it assumes a steady weekly schedule. Add overtime hours manually if they are regular.

Is the result before or after tax?

+
Before tax (gross). Use the take-home pay calculator for your net income.

Can I use it for part-time work?

+
Yes — just enter your actual weekly hours and it scales the salary accordingly.

Yes, free with no sign-up, and private in your browser.
