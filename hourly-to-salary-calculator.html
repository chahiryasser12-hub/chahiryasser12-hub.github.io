Quick answer

This calculator gives an instant estimate from the values entered above. Use the result as a planning starting point, then review the assumptions and limitations.

How to use the calculator

Enter the requested values using consistent units. Change one input at a time when comparing scenarios, and verify every value before using the result.

Assumptions

The estimate uses the values currently entered and the formula described on this page. Any fees, taxes, timing rules or personal circumstances not requested are excluded.

Common mistakes

  • Mixing monthly and annual values
  • Using inconsistent measurement units
  • Changing several inputs at once when comparing scenarios
  • Treating an estimate as a quote, approval, diagnosis or guarantee

What this calculator does

This tool converts an hourly rate into weekly, monthly and annual gross pay.

Formula used

Annual gross pay = hourly rate × paid hours per week × paid weeks per year.

Worked example

At $20 per hour, 40 hours a week and 52 paid weeks, estimated annual gross pay is $41,600.

How to interpret the result

The result is gross pay before deductions. Changing unpaid weeks or weekly hours can significantly change annual income.

Important limitations

Overtime premiums, bonuses, unpaid leave, taxes, benefits and irregular schedules are excluded unless reflected in the inputs. Currency is a display unit.

Frequently asked questions

Does the estimate include tax?

+
No. It converts gross hourly pay before deductions.

What if I do not work 52 paid weeks?

+
Enter the number of paid weeks that reflects unpaid leave or seasonal work.

Is overtime included?

+
Only if you incorporate overtime hours and the correct overtime rate separately.
