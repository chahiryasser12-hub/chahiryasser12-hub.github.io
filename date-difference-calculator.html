Quick answer

Date Difference calculator gives an instant estimate from the values entered above. Use the result as a starting point and review the assumptions and limits below.

How to use the calculator

Enter valid dates in the requested order and check the counting convention.

Assumptions

Calendar calculations follow the browser calendar and do not determine legal deadlines.

Common mistakes

  • Not stating inclusive or exclusive counting
  • Mixing dates and timestamps
  • Ignoring leap days
  • Using an estimate for a legal deadline

What this calculator does

This calculator measures elapsed time between two calendar dates.

Formula used

It subtracts the earlier date from the later date and reports the exact day count, with a calendar breakdown where available.

Worked example

From January 1 to January 31 is 30 elapsed days when the start date is excluded.

How to interpret the result

The displayed result helps distinguish elapsed days from inclusive counting.

Important limitations

Results can differ if both endpoints should be counted. Time of day, time zones and daylight-saving changes are not included in date-only calculations.
