What this calculator does

BMI compares an adult’s weight with the square of height and is commonly used as a screening measure.

Formula used

BMI = weight in kilograms ÷ height in metres².

Worked example

For 72 kg and 1.75 m: BMI = 72 ÷ (1.75 × 1.75) = 23.5.

How to interpret the result

For adults, 23.5 falls in the healthy-weight screening range of 18.5 to less than 25.

Important limitations

BMI does not directly measure body fat and cannot distinguish fat, muscle and bone. It should be considered with other health information. Adult categories are not the correct interpretation for children or teens, and the result is not a diagnosis.
