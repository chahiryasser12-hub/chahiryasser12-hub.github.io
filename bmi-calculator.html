The BMI formula

BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)². The most widely used quick screen for a healthy weight.

What the categories mean

Under 18.5 underweight, 18.5–24.9 healthy, 25–29.9 overweight, 30+ obese (WHO).

Why BMI isn't the whole story

BMI doesn't tell muscle from fat. A muscular athlete can read 'overweight' while lean. Use it as a starting point.

FAQ

Is BMI accurate for everyone? +
A useful screen for most adults, less so for athletes, elderly or pregnancy.
What's a healthy BMI? +
Generally 18.5–24.9.
