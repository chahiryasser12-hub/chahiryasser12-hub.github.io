The BMI formula

BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)². Someone who is 175 cm and 72 kg has a BMI of 23.5, in the healthy range. It is the most widely used quick screen for a healthy weight for your height.

What the categories mean

Under 18.5 underweight, 18.5–24.9 healthy, 25–29.9 overweight, 30+ obese (WHO). To turn a target weight into a daily plan, our calorie calculator gives you a maintenance and goal calorie figure.

Why BMI isn't the whole story

BMI doesn't tell muscle from fat. A muscular athlete can read overweight while lean. Pair it with our body fat calculator for a fuller picture.
