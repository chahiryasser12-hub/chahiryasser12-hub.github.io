What this calculator does

BMI compares an adult’s weight with the square of height and is commonly used as a screening measure.

Formula used

BMI = weight in kilograms ÷ height in metres².

Worked example

For 72 kg and 1.75 m: BMI = 72 ÷ (1.75 × 1.75) = 23.5.

How to interpret the result

For adults, 23.5 falls in the healthy-weight screening range of 18.5 to less than 25.

Important limitations

BMI does not directly measure body fat and cannot distinguish fat, muscle and bone. It should be considered with other health information. Adult categories are not the correct interpretation for children or teens, and the result is not a diagnosis.

Frequently asked questions

What does my BMI result mean?

+
For adults, the result is compared with general screening ranges. It should be considered with other health information.

Is BMI accurate for athletes?

+
BMI may classify muscular people higher because it cannot distinguish muscle, fat and bone.

Can children use adult BMI categories?

+
No. Children and teenagers require age- and sex-specific growth charts.

When should I speak to a professional?

+
Speak with a qualified healthcare professional if you have concerns about weight, nutrition, symptoms or major lifestyle changes.
