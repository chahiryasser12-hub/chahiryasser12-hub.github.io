## How to research (free)
1. **Google Keyword Planner** — real Google volume + competition.
2. **Ubersuggest / Answer the Public** — long-tail + question ideas (3 free/day).
3. **Google autocomplete + "People also ask"** — free goldmine of long-tails.
4. Check the **top 10 results**: if they're weak/old, you can rank.
