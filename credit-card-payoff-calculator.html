Credit Card Payoff Calculator

Estimate credit card payoff time, total interest and the effect of an extra monthly payment.

How to read this result

The payoff estimate assumes no new charges or fees and a constant APR and monthly payment.

How payoff is estimated

The calculator applies monthly interest, subtracts the entered payment and repeats until the balance reaches zero.

Minimum-payment warning

A payment that does not exceed monthly interest cannot reduce the balance in this simplified model.

Important limitation

Real cards can use daily compounding, variable APRs, fees and changing minimum payments.

Frequently asked questions

How long will it take to pay off a credit card?

+
It depends on the balance, APR, payment and whether new charges are added.

Does an extra payment save interest?

+
Usually yes because the balance declines faster.

What if the payment is below monthly interest?

+
The simplified payoff calculation cannot produce a payoff date.

Does this include new purchases?

+
No. It assumes no new charges or fees.
