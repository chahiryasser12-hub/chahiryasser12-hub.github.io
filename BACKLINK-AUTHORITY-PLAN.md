# 90-day backlink and authority plan

## Principles
- Earn links to genuinely useful calculators and guides.
- Prefer relevant websites with real audiences over bulk directory submissions.
- Never buy link packages, exchange links at scale or post repetitive comments.

## Month 1: establish the entity
- Complete consistent Fynzo profiles on GitHub, LinkedIn, Pinterest and relevant product/tool directories.
- Submit five high-quality tool listings with different, accurate descriptions.
- Contact five resource-page owners whose audience can genuinely use a specific Fynzo calculator.
- Track every outreach target, response, published link and destination page.

## Month 2: create linkable assets
- Publish two data-rich comparison guides and one clear methodology page.
- Pitch the mortgage comparison, percentage examples and calculator methodology to niche bloggers and educators.
- Answer useful community questions without forcing a link.

## Month 3: consolidate authority
- Review Search Console pages receiving impressions and direct outreach to the strongest pages.
- Reclaim unlinked Fynzo mentions and ask for the brand name to link to the relevant page.
- Target five new referring domains, not five links from one domain.

## Monthly KPI
- New relevant referring domains
- Links to calculators rather than only the homepage
- Referral sessions and engaged sessions
- Organic impressions and non-brand queries
- Removed, broken or low-quality links
