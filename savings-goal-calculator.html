How Much to Save Per Month — Savings Goal Calculator

How it works

It accounts for what you already have (and its interest) then works out the monthly amount needed by your target date.

Make it realistic

If the monthly figure feels too high, extend your deadline or lower the target.

FAQ

Does it include interest? +
Yes — growth on both current savings and future contributions.
What return should I use? +
1–4% for savings; more for investments, but nothing is guaranteed.
