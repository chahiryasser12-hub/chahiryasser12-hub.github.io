# How to add the blog to your existing repo (nothing deleted)

Upload ALL these files to your repo ROOT (drag & drop in GitHub):

- `blog.html`            → replaces your "Guides coming soon" page (10-post index + featured)
- `blog-*.html` (10)     → the 10 full articles
- `blog.css`             → blog styles (linked automatically by the pages)
- `og-blog-*.png` (10)   → social share images for each post
- `hero.svg`             → REPLACES your empty homepage hero with a full 3-card illustration

That's it. Your nav already has a "Blog" link → it now shows 10 real posts.

## Why this matters for AdSense
AdSense wants real, original content. 10 useful guides (each linking to a calculator,
each with its own image + Article schema) is exactly the kind of content that gets sites approved.

## Optional

- Your existing blog auto-publisher can keep adding posts on top of these.
