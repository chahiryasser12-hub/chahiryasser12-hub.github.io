What this calculator includes

The financed amount can include sales tax and fees after subtracting the trade-in and cash down payment.

Comparison tip

Compare the total interest and total payment, not only the monthly payment. A longer term can reduce the payment while increasing borrowing cost.

Important limitation

Dealer calculations can treat trade-ins, tax, rebates and fees differently. Confirm the purchase order and lender disclosure.

Frequently asked questions

Does the calculation include sales tax?

+
Yes, using the entered percentage and the displayed formula assumptions.

Can I include a trade-in?

+
Yes. The trade-in value reduces the price before the illustrative sales-tax calculation.

Does a longer term cost more?

+
At the same rate and principal, a longer term usually lowers the monthly payment and increases total interest.

Is this a lender quote?

+
No. It is an educational estimate.
