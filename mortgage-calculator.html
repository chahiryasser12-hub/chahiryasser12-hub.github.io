How the mortgage calculator works

This calculator applies the standard amortization formula banks use. It takes your loan amount (home price minus down payment), spreads it over your term, and shows your monthly principal-and-interest payment plus total interest. Everything runs instantly in your browser, so you can compare a 30-year loan against a 15-year loan in real time.

What is included (PITI)

The result covers principal and interest. Property taxes, homeowners insurance and PMI vary by location, so add them on top. Lenders like total housing cost under about 28% of gross monthly income. If you are unsure what you can borrow, our loan calculator helps you compare monthly costs across different amounts and terms.

Tips to lower your payment

  • Increase your down payment to shrink the loan and avoid PMI at 20%.
  • Shorten the term to cut total interest.
  • Improve your credit score for a better rate.
