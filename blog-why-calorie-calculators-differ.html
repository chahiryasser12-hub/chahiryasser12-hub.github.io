Why Do Calorie Calculators Give Different Results?

August 1, 2026Reviewed by Yasser Chahir

Calorie estimates differ because equations, activity multipliers, rounding and personal energy expenditure are not identical.

Different BMR equations

Mifflin-St Jeor and revised Harris-Benedict can produce different starting estimates.

Activity multipliers

Two websites can label activity levels differently, causing larger TDEE differences than the BMR formula.

Individual variation

Daily movement, body composition, sleep, health and food tracking can make real needs differ from a formula.

Practical takeaway

Use a calculator as a starting estimate, track a stable routine over time, and adjust with appropriate professional support when health circumstances require it.
